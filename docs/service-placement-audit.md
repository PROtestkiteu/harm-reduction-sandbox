# Service placement audit

New games begin with seven unplaced services. Equipment is available through Services, but operating history, map labels and physical setup begin only after the first site is confirmed. A cancelled or previewed location does not establish a service.

| Service | First site | Before confirmation | After choosing another site |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drop-in | Existing building | Ordinary home shell | Frontage at selected building, home stays ordinary |
| Drug checking | Existing building | Ordinary home shell | Frontage at selected building, home stays ordinary |
| Shelter | Existing building | Ordinary home shell | Frontage at selected building, home stays ordinary |
| Clinic | Existing building | Ordinary home shell | Frontage at selected building, home stays ordinary |
| Club harm reduction | Existing building or the club site | Nightclub business remains, player stand absent | Service frontage at chosen building, original player stand absent |
| Foot patrol | Free curb | Table and service label absent | Table, staff positions and routes use chosen curb |
| Outreach | Free curb | Camper and service label absent | Camper assembles at chosen curb while time may remain paused |

Hosted frontage appears immediately. Mobile curbs exclude other committed service sites, including legacy parked campers. Relocating a camper updates its saved site. Neutral shells share geometry with their existing buildings and are never drawn alongside the corresponding branded home.

The tutorial first introduces the camp, then guides stand setup through Services before dispatch. Every added task uses the same brief-before-clue contract. Prior operated services and committed sites in saves remain established; closing a service does not erase its placement history.

Validation: service-site domain/save tests, all seven normal UI placement/cancel paths, desktop and touch bounds, full tutorial, render probe and relevant walking-clearance checks. Physical Safari, phones and Quest require device testing.
